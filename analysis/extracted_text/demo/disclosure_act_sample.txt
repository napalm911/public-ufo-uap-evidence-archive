UAP Disclosure Act of 2023

This section establishes procedures for the review and potential declassification
of records related to unidentified anomalous phenomena held by U.S. government agencies.

The Archivist of the United States shall establish a UAP Records Collection
at the National Archives for documents transferred under this act.

Senator Schumer and Senator Rounds introduced bipartisan legislation requiring
an independent review board for UAP-related records.
